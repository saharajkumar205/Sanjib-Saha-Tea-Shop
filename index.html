Our Story

For over 50 years, our shop has served the Santipur community as a trusted destination for quality loose tea and daily essentials.

We supply carefully selected CTC and loose tea leaf blends for rich, full-bodied home brewing, alongside trusted packaged dairy milk powder. We also stock durable food plates and bowls for family events, gatherings, and local catering needs—all available at fair retail and bulk rates.
