Our Story

Started over 25 years ago by my father, our shop began with a simple belief: a good cup of tea can reset your whole day.

Every morning before dawn, we hand-crush green cardamom, fresh ginger, and select whole spices to blend with premium tea leaves. Whether you are stepping out for a morning start or taking a well-deserved evening break with friends, you will always get a hot, fresh, and soul-satisfying cup.
